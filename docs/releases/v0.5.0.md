## The story

Through v0.4.x the library was overwhelmingly *algebraic*: substrate constants, the K₇/Spin(7) structure, mass and coupling formulae, and the benchmark/closure tooling that audits them. The carriers — the knotted field configurations those numbers are supposed to *describe* — were drawn (`diagrams`) but not computed.

v0.5.0 closes that gap. It ships the **construct/measure layer** of NWT's field content: the code that builds a carrier field configuration and reads physical quantities off it — Hopf charge, energy, form factor, linking number — without yet minimising anything. The energy-minimising relaxers (the expensive, GPU-shaped half) live in the separate **`jax-solitons`** engine; the relationship is one-way, and deliberately so: these pure-numpy primitives are that engine's **reference oracle**, and as of this release a live cross-engine test pins this package and checks the two independent implementations agree to ~1e-16 (so neither can silently drift). Nothing here changes a published number; it adds the machinery the next round of papers measures *with*.

## 1. Faddeev-Skyrme (Hopf) solitons — `solitons.faddeev`

The forward, diagnostic half of NWT's L₃ Skyrme-Faddeev sector (Paper 16), pure-numpy and rendering-free:

- `n_from_Z` — the CP¹ → S² map `nᵃ = Z†σᵃZ / Z†Z`.
- `solid_angle_triangle` / `plaquette_area_form` — the **faithful Berg-Lüscher area form**: the signed spherical area an S² field sweeps per lattice plaquette (two Van Oosterom-Strackee triangles). Summed over a closed surface it is exactly 2π·(integer), which is what supplies the lattice unwinding *barrier* the naive same-index `∂ᵢn×∂ⱼn` density lacks — that one is only O(dx)-accurate and lets a discrete hopfion unwind to vacuum at every resolution.
- `whitehead_hopf_charge` — the **field-theoretic** Whitehead Hopf charge `Q_H = (1/16π²)∫A·B` (B from the area form; `curl A = B` solved spectrally in Coulomb gauge). Distinct from the algebraic carrier-knot `topology.hopf_charge(p, m) = p·m`; they coincide in value for the field that realises a given knot but are computed from entirely different data.
- `rational_hopfion` — a **smooth, compact, in-basin** hopfion seed (Battye-Sutcliffe / Hietarinta-Salo rational map): no 0/0 spinor singularity on the core ring, and it reaches the south-pole vacuum *exactly* beyond a finite tube radius (periodic-safe), with charge `Q_H = n·m`.
- `faddeev_energy` / `energy_density` (forward-difference E₂ — no Nyquist null space — plus the area-form E₄) and `soliton_size` (energy-weighted RMS radius + the E₂/E₄ virial split).

Together these stabilised the **first lattice hopfion in the project**: `Q_H` holds at +0.998 where every prior seed unwound to Q→0, energy converges as O(dx²), and the Derrick `E ∝ √c₄` scaling holds to 0.02%. 15 tests.

## 2. Classical EM + form factors — `em`, `amplitudes`

The bridge from a carrier's charge/supercurrent density to its observable electromagnetic signature:

- **`em`** — FFT-Poisson solvers for the fields a carrier radiates: `electric_field` (∇²φ = −ρ), `magnetic_field` (∇²A = −μ₀j, Biot-Savart), `divergence`/`curl`, source deposition, field-line tracing, and `multipole_moments`. Both `bc="periodic"` (neutralizing background, Gauss-law-clean near the source) and `bc="open"` (zero-padded free-space Green's function — no image charges). `maxwell_eh` adds the optional **Euler-Heisenberg** nonlinear-vacuum correction (`L_EH = ξ[(E²−B²)² + 7(E·B)²]`), with a convergence guard that stops early on `tol` and warns if pushed into the non-convergent strong-field regime.
- **`amplitudes` + `em` form factors** — `form_factor` (spherically-averaged elastic |F(q)|, F(0)=1; open-BC option for clean low-q), `mean_square_radius` (signed ⟨r²⟩ — negative allowed for a net-neutral neutron-like distribution), `mott_cross_section`, and `elastic_form_factor_cross_section` (`dσ/dΩ = (dσ/dΩ)_Mott·|F(q²)|²`). The full charge-density → form-factor → scattering chain, for testing NWT carrier form factors against the proton dipole / neutron Galster shapes.

## 3. Gauged Ginzburg-Landau vortices at any κ — `condensate.solve_gl_vortex`

Generalises the self-dual `solve_bps_vortex` (κ=1, first-order shooting) to the full second-order GL / de Vega-Schaposnik system by boundary-value collocation: scalar `f(ρ)` + gauge `a(ρ)` for any `κ = λ/ξ` and any winding `n`. κ=1 reproduces the BPS point to <5e-3; κ<1 type-I, κ>1 type-II. The collocation solver is **stable for n ≥ 2**, closing the gap the BPS shooting left open. Returns a `GLVortexProfile` with `f_at`/`a_at` interpolators and a `supercurrent()` helper for the screening sheath `f²(n−a)/ρ` (whose radius migrates outward with κ) — the gauge-invariant answer to "where does a carrier's framing charge sit radially?".

## 4. Gauss / Milnor link invariants — `topology.linking_invariants`

The numeric primitive behind NWT's "binding = Hopf linking of carrier knots" picture (Paper 20), on point-sampled closed curves:

- `gauss_linking_number` / `linking_matrix` (total linking = `.sum()/2` — the deuteron a single clasp lk=−1, the alpha four trefoils with all six K₄ edges clasped, total +6).
- `link_deletion_test` + `borromean_rings()` — separate Borromean from "anti-Borromean" links.
- `milnor_indeterminacy(L)` — gcd of the pairwise linkings, the modulus of the triple Milnor (Massey/Borromean) invariant: 0 ⇒ Borromean regime, 1 ⇒ a spatial Massey invariant is obstructed (the alpha's all-+1 links).
- `tetrahedral_rotations()` + `link_symmetry_permutations` — rotational symmetry detection (the alpha cluster's full A₄).

Rendering-free by design, so a downstream portrait/compendium library can build on it; consolidates a Gauss-linking integral that was duplicated across three private scripts. 21 tests.

## 5. Particle portraits — `portraits`

Renders each NWT particle from its *actual field content*: the exact BPS Nielsen-Olesen vortex profile (`condensate.solve_bps_vortex`) bent along the carrier-knot curve, with the abelian-Higgs phase `p·φ + q·θ` composited as an emission-absorption volume. `portrait(p, q)` / `portrait(hopf=True)` / `gallery()` render the carrier zoo (unknot/lepton, Hopf/meson, trefoil/baryon, …). `diagrams.torus_knot_curve` / `hopf_link_curves` now also *return* their curves, not just draw them.

## 6. Benchmark closure-priority + two derived-not-measured fixes

- **`o10.closure_priority` / `--priority`** — the directional dual of the diagnostic layers: not "what breaks?" but "where does the next month of effort buy the most structural closure?". Suite sits at 23.5 / 38 (62%); open items split into effort-known (ROI-ranked) and an honest triage queue.
- **`benchmark_muon_decay_rate`** no longer uses the measured PDG `G_F` (itself extracted from τ_μ — the source of a circular 0.000%); on the substrate Fermi constant + substrate α it lands at a genuine 0.011% vs PDG.
- **QED cross sections** now close on the *derived* α (`isa.ALPHA_SUBSTRATE`) rather than a hardcoded CODATA literal in the matrix elements, dropping hidden single-points-of-failure 5 → 1.

## Engine ↔ oracle factoring (housekeeping)

This release also formalises the boundary with `jax-solitons`: the `solitons.faddeev` docstrings now state the accurate relationship (the engine is *validated against* these primitives, not *importing* them), and a live cross-engine equivalence gate on the engine side pins this package and asserts agreement on energy and Hopf charge — plus a code-quality pass that moved all structural integers in the touched shims onto `isa.constants` and added a CodeQL workflow.

## What's next
