## The story

v0.3.0 shipped a 38-benchmark reproducibility suite and invited criticism. Three independent reviews arrived on d12rg, and this release is the response to all three — plus a full self-consistency audit of the library that the reviews prompted. The throughline: make the prediction/measurement boundary, and the structure behind each number, explicit and checkable rather than asserted.

## 1. Correction layers where benchmarks were leading-order (P. Kaboth)

Pasquale flagged the two largest deviations as the interesting cases — "where additional coupling or correction layers may be required." The diagnosis differed:

- **Z-boson width** was pure leading-order (2.93% low). The hadronic channels now carry the standard massless-quark QCD radiative correction `R_QCD = 1 + a + 1.409 a² − 12.77 a³` (`a = α_s/π`). **Γ_Z: 2.4223 → 2.4874 GeV (2.93% → 0.31%)**; BR(Z→ℓℓ) moves from 3.443% toward the PDG 3.366%.
- **Muon lifetime** (9.94%) was *not* a missing weak layer: because Γ_μ ∝ G_F² m_μ⁵, the benchmark was amplifying the Paper-6 mass residual fivefold (m_μ is −1.97%; ×5 ≈ 9.8%). The isolated weak-sector closure (substrate G_F + PDG m_μ + the standard phase-space and 1-loop QED radiative correction) is now **0.007%**; the compound benchmark is re-labeled and its deviation correctly attributed to the mass formula.

## 2. Sensitivity → structural criticality (M. Wende)

Marcel observed that *sensitivity* (how strongly an observable responds) is not the only quantity of interest — *structural criticality* (which integers carry the most load, which observables co-move) reveals the architecture. `SensitivityReport` gains `structural_load()` (integers ranked by how many observables they move), `comovement()` (benchmark pairs that move together = correlated dependencies), and a `python -m nwt_substrate.sensitivity --criticality` view.

## 3. O10 — the derivation-separation boundary (L. Leighton)

Implements the DAG cit-readout specialization of L. Leighton's O10 Ladder Derivation Protocol:

- `nwt_substrate.benchmarks.predict` — a standalone emitter of dimensionless substrate predictions that takes **no input of any kind** (α is the closed form `1/(25π√3+1)`; every value is a pure function of the K_7/Spin(7) integers). Measured values are quarantined as a separate `--reference` stream; per the O10 constants rule the witness layer is **CODATA-2018** (post-SI2019 *defined* constants excluded). `diff -u` of the two streams is the cit readout.
- `nwt_substrate.benchmarks.o10` — the whole DAG that rung lives in, as a validated structure: proof-order edges are one-way, the graph is acyclic, witnesses are sinks, and the commutative-diagram identities (e.g. `21 = C(7,2) = 3·7`) are exactly isa's import-time asserts. `build_suite_dag()` extends this to the **full 38-benchmark suite** — the cit readout is then the structurally-enforced falsification report (27 admissible, 8 defect edges *marked*, 3 qualitative), and its `STRUCTURAL→OUTPUT` edges are the *computed* sensitivity coupling, so the DAG's load ranking equals the sweep's. `python -m nwt_substrate.benchmarks.o10 --suite [--sensitivity]`.

This makes the contamination that produced the v0.3.0 sin²θ_W bug — a measured effective angle reported as the prediction — structurally impossible, and makes plain that sin²θ_W = (2+α)/9 is a ~3.5% leading-order on-shell angle, not a sub-1% result.

## 4. Self-consistency audit (library-internal, value-preserving)

A full audit against the canonical v0.3.1 `isa/constants.py`:

- **Fixed**: a `dark_sector` WIMP docstring contradicting its own formula/tests; `string.is_trefoil` returning True for the unknot; an inconsistent `gravity.healing_length` docstring; a false `particles.stability_ratio` docstring; 26 stale ghost exports in `electroweak.__all__`; `amplitudes.vertices.G_WEAK` single-sourced from `electroweak.G_W`; a latent `amplitudes↔electroweak↔qcd` circular import.
- **Changed**: the neutrino NNLO Wilson bracket unified on the canonical additive `(1 + α/7 + (21/8)α²)` form.
- **Added**: `isa.N_TRIANGLES_K7 = C(7,3) = 35` (import-time asserted), closing an orphan the chemistry f-block rule depended on; ~a dozen bare structural literals routed through `isa`; `K7_CORRECTION_KCAL_PER_RING_SET`, `STEANE_LZ`, and `G_F` de-duplicated.
